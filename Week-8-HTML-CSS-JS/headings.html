CS50x

CS50x is Harvard University's introduction to computer science and the art of programming, offered free online to learners around the world. It's an adaptation of the on-campus CS50 course and covers a broad range of foundational topics, including abstraction, algorithms, data structures, memory management, software engineering, and web development. Along the way, students work with languages like C, Python, and SQL, plus tools such as HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

Abt

Throughout the course, students complete problem sets inspired by real-world domains and ultimately build a final project of their own design. CS50x is self-paced and welcoming to students of all backgrounds, whether or not they have prior programming experience. Upon completion, learners can earn a certificate. You can find full details on the official course website at https://cs50.harvard.edu.
